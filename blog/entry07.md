## Takeaways from My In-Class Presentation
One takeaway from my in-class presentation was the importance of preparation. Practicing beforehand helped me explain my project more clearly and confidently. I learned that when I know my content well, I can focus more on engaging with the audience instead of worrying about what to say next.

Another takeaway was that visual design matters when presenting a website. My classmates were more interested when I demonstrated interactive elements, images, and animations. This showed me that a strong visual presentation can help communicate ideas more effectively.

I also learned that feedback is valuable. Questions from classmates helped me think about improvements I could make to my website and future projects.


## Takeaways from My Expo Elevator Pitch
During the Expo, I had to explain my project to different visitors in a short amount of time. This taught me how to summarize my project quickly while still highlighting the most important features.

One thing that went well was explaining my future technology ideas. Many visitors were interested in hearing about how graphic design might change through AI, holographic displays, and virtual reality. Their interest showed me that innovation and creativity can make a project more engaging.

The Expo also helped me improve my communication skills. Since I spoke with many different people, I became more comfortable answering questions and adjusting my explanations based on the audience.
