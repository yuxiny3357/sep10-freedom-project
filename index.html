Graphic Design

Past • Present • Future

Overview

Graphic design has existed for thousands of years, beginning with early visual communication such as hieroglyphics in ancient Egypt. Over time, the tools and methods used by designers have evolved from simple materials like clay and papyrus into advanced digital software and hardware.

Part A: Existing Technology

Ancient Tools

Clay tablets and papyrus scrolls were used to record and communicate ideas. These early tools show how visual communication began and how information was preserved.

Modern Apps

Apps like PicMonkey, GIMP, and Visme provide powerful tools for editing, creating graphics, and designing layouts. These tools make design more accessible and efficient.

Devices

Traditional Devices

Lightboxes and drafting tables helped artists sketch and trace designs with precision before digital tools existed.

Modern Devices

Drawing tablets, scanners, and external SSDs improve digital workflows and allow artists to create, store, and edit designs efficiently.

Future Technology

Holographic Canvas

This future tool could allow designers to create 3D visuals in real time using gestures, making design more interactive and immersive.

Dream Printer

The dream printer could instantly print designs with advanced materials, improving speed and creative possibilities.
